第3页 00:45–01:20（35秒）
【说课】本课来自人教版高中英语选择性必修第一册，第三十八至三十九页。虽然标题中有 Listening，但这里是一篇阅读语篇，标题通过拟人突出身体也能表达意义。内容围绕文化差异、共同例子和微笑的多种作用展开。教学由标题预测、结构梳理和细节比较，走向给访华朋友 Tim 提建议，帮助学生将阅读信息转化为有语境的交际表达。

第4页 01:20–01:55（35秒）
【说课】学生通常有手势的生活经验和基本阅读能力，但可能凭直觉判断含义，或者记住国家名称却混淆动作与含义，也可能把个别文化例子当成所有人的规则。我因此提供动作、含义、语境三栏表，以及情境、建议、理由句框。较熟练的学生进一步比较场景和限定表达。学情是备课假设，真实教学中还需用入口回答与任务单检验。

第5页 01:55–02:35（40秒）
【说课】目标依据课件的三项学习目标展开，并对应英语学科四个维度。语言能力落实为结构理解、细节提取和建议表达；思维品质强调引用文本和限定结论；文化意识强调尊重差异；学习能力通过伙伴核对和修改建议体现。评价证据包括六个段落功能、至少四项准确文化比较、至少四种微笑用途，以及三条带情境和理由的建议，使目标能够被观察和检查。

第6页 02:35–03:10（35秒）
【说课】重点是根据主题句和例子读懂结构，比较差异与相似性，并将阅读所得用于建议。难点是同时关联动作、含义和语境，避免文化泛化，再形成合适的表达。突破方式是示范一项表格记录、追问文本依据、引导伙伴核验，最后用情境加建议加理由的句框修改过于绝对的表述。

第8页 03:40–04:15（35秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Good morning, everyone. Watch me without listening to any words.
【操作】教师先挥手，再将双手并拢放在脸颊旁，闭眼，演示“hello”与“sleep”；无需媒体播放。
What did you notice? What might these movements mean?
【操作】停顿2秒后点击呈现观察与解释。

第9页 04:15–05:00（45秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Look at the title: “Listening to How Bodies Talk”. Can bodies really talk with words? Think for a moment.
【操作】停顿2秒，点击呈现 personification。
The writer gives bodies a human action. This is personification. It suggests that movements can communicate ideas and feelings. Which prediction best fits the title and picture: only similarities, or body language in different cultures?
【操作】点击揭示 D，强调预测并非最终结论。
D is our best prediction. Let us read to check it. A prediction starts our reading; the text will provide the evidence.

第10页 05:00–06:00（60秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Skim the passage and focus on the topic sentences. Match each paragraph to its function.
【操作】停顿2秒；点击呈现六项核对答案。
Our matches are one B, two A, three F, four C, five D, and six E. B summarises paragraph one; it is not the exact opening sentence. Find the supporting ideas about words and body language expressing thoughts and feelings.
Now group the paragraphs. Paragraph one introduces the topic. Paragraphs two to four show cultural differences. Paragraph five gives similarities. Paragraph six explains different uses.
【操作】依次点击显示四部分结构。
Topic sentences and examples work together. We now have a map for detailed reading. Our title prediction is supported, and the passage also explores shared examples and multiple uses.

第11页 06:00–07:05（65秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Read paragraph two. What can eye contact mean? Record the gesture, meaning, and context together.
【操作】指向第一条原文，停顿2秒后点击示范答案。
In some countries, eye contact displays interest. The passage does not name these countries, so we should not add names. It also gives an example of eye contact between men and women not being socially approved in many Middle Eastern countries. Keep that particular context in your answer.
Now read the sentence about Japan. Looking down when speaking to an older person may demonstrate respect.
【操作】依次点击另外两条表格记录。
Does this mean every person in a country behaves in the same way? No. These are the passage’s cultural examples. Relationships and situations also matter. Accurate reading includes the context and words such as “may”.

第12页 07:05–08:00（55秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Now scan paragraphs three and four. The same gesture may carry different meanings. Find the OK sign and compare the examples.
【操作】停顿2秒后点击呈现OK表格。
In the passage, it can mean money in Japan and zero in France. It is not considered polite in Brazil and Germany. One gesture does not carry one fixed meaning.
What about yes and no? In the comparison about Bulgaria and southern Albania, shaking the head means yes and nodding means no. Keep both the action and the meaning in your notes.
【操作】点击呈现P4例子。
Kissing a friend on the cheek is a greeting in countries such as France and Russia. Friendship is part of that example. Compare with evidence, rather than guessing from your own habits.

第13页 08:00–08:40（40秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Are all meanings different? Read paragraph five. Hands together beside the head, with eyes closed, suggest sleep. A hand moving in circles over the stomach after a meal means “I am full” in the example.
【操作】依次点击两项答案。
Look carefully at “Some gestures seem to have the same meaning everywhere.” What does “seem” tell us?
【操作】停顿2秒，点击呈现谨慎结论。
The writer presents broadly shared examples. We should still check the situation before treating a meaning as universal. Differences and similarities can both help us understand one another.

第14页 08:40–09:30（50秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Read paragraph six. A smile has many uses. Find at least four uses and point to the supporting words.
【操作】停顿2秒后按两类用途点击显示答案。
A smile can help us find friends, break down barriers, apologise, greet someone, ask for help, or start a conversation. It can also help us get through difficult situations, feel happier and stronger, and give comfort to a friend who feels down or lonely.
How does this support the final part of our structure?
【操作】停顿2秒。
One form of body language can serve several purposes. To understand a smile, notice who is smiling, to whom, and in what situation.

第15页 09:30–10:25（55秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Our friend Tim is visiting China for the first time. Use what you have learnt to give him advice for three situations: meeting people, talking to others, and sitting or standing in a formal setting.
Use this frame: “When you …, you could … because …”. Work with a partner and prepare at least three points. Include a situation and a reason for each.
【操作】点击呈现句框；无生试讲停顿2秒，不等待真实课堂的合作准备时间。
For a first meeting, an appropriate handshake or nod may be a good choice. Follow your host’s lead, because greetings depend on the relationship and setting. Advice should help Tim make a choice, rather than give him an absolute rule for every person in China.

第16页 10:25–11:25（60秒）
【无生试讲｜英语】Here is a short model message for Tim.
【操作】教师示范；依次点击三条建议，不虚构学生已经完成任务。
Hi, Tim. At a first meeting, an appropriate handshake or nod may be suitable because greetings depend on people and settings. Follow your host’s lead. When talking, use comfortable eye contact without staring, because it can help you show attention. In a formal meeting, sit or stand attentively, because it helps you show that you are listening.
Now let us improve this sentence: “Everyone in China always shakes hands.”
【操作】停顿2秒后点击显示修改。
“At a first meeting, an appropriate handshake or nod may be suitable.” What improved? We added a situation and removed an absolute claim. Crossed legs or folded arms alone do not prove disrespect, either. Check the context and revise one sentence in your own message.

第17页 11:25–12:20（55秒）

【操作】四个分支按顺序点击显示；指向关键词串联内容。

What should we do when meanings are unfamiliar? Learn about the culture, understand the situation, and respect differences.

【操作】停顿2秒后教师给出示范。

第19页 13:10–14:35（85秒）
【教学反思｜说课】对本次设计与试讲，我从三个方面反思。第一，标题预测、结构图、比较表和建议任务构成连续的学习链条，能帮助阅读由找信息走向运用信息。但是否真正达成，要在真实课堂检查学生能否同时记录动作、含义和语境，并说出依据，而不是只看表格是否填满。第二，文化比较容易被简化成固定标签。后续应检查建议中的 always、everyone 等词，观察学生能否补充人物关系、场合和 may 等限定表达。第三，学生阅读速度与表达基础有差异。需要支持的学生可先使用关键词和句框，较熟练的学生比较不同场景，并解释一次修改。若建议只在重复教师范句，我会增加“这个建议何时不合适”的追问。后续以任务单、修改前后建议和出口句收集证据，再调整支架与时间。这些反思依据设计和无生试讲，不能代替真实课堂的学习成效证据。
